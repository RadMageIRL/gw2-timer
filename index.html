        GW2 Timer v0.1.0

        A Guild Wars 2 timer for world bosses and meta-events: what is in its scheduled window now, what starts next, in your local time. Unofficial fansite, not affiliated with ArenaNet or NCSOFT.

        Scheduled, not live

        Every time here is a scheduled start. The game publishes no live state for events, so a map can run early, late, fail, or close. "Happening now" means the scheduled window is open, not that the fight is on in your map.

        Schedule data

        Most times agree across at least two independent sources. A few carry a label:

        • Unverified: new, or the sources disagree. If you see it start at another time, use Adjust on that event to set the time you saw. Your setting stays in this browser.
        • Recheck: a game update may have changed it; the time shown is the previous schedule until it is checked.

        Sources: the Guild Wars 2 Wiki's event timer data, ArenaNet's game update notes and news, and the official Guild Wars 2 API for waypoint codes. Times were also compared with an independent community schedule (qjv/event-timers).

        Privacy

        Settings, stars and adjustments are saved in this browser only. Two things leave it: the clock check asks this site's own address for the time, and the news panel loads headlines from guildwars2.com, so ArenaNet sees that request. You can turn news off.
