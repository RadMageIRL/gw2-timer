        How sure each time is

          Documented
          At least two independent written sources agree on the time. This is a documentation check, not an in-game check.
          Confirmed in game
          The start was seen in game and matched the schedule within two minutes.
          Disputed
          Sources disagree on the time. Labeled Unverified on the event, with Adjust.
          Unverified
          New or thinly sourced, and not yet checked in game. Labeled Unverified, with Adjust.
          Recheck
          A game update may have changed the time. The previous schedule shows until it is checked.

          Uncertain times

            If you see one of these start at another time, use Adjust on that event to set the time you saw. Your setting stays in this browser.

              GW2 Timer v0.3.0

              A Guild Wars 2 timer for world bosses and meta-events: what is in its scheduled window now, what starts next, in your local time. Unofficial fansite, not affiliated with ArenaNet or NCSOFT.

              Scheduled, not live

              Every time here is a scheduled start. The game publishes no live state for events, so a map can run early, late, fail, or close. "Scheduled now" means the scheduled window is open, not that the fight is on in your map.

              Schedule data

              Most times agree across at least two independent sources. A few carry a label (Schedule notes, at the bottom of the page, lists them):

              • Unverified: new, or the sources disagree. If you see it start at another time, use Adjust on that event to set the time you saw. Your setting stays in this browser.
              • Recheck: a game update may have changed it; the time shown is the previous schedule until it is checked.

              Sources: the Guild Wars 2 Wiki's event timer data, ArenaNet's game update notes and news, and the official Guild Wars 2 API for waypoint codes. Times were also compared with an independent community schedule (qjv/event-timers).

              Reminders

              Reminders are off until you turn them on with the Reminders button by the filters. Then a notice appears on this page, with a count in the tab's title, 5, 10 or 15 minutes before a starred event starts. A reminder is not a notification from your browser or your computer, and it comes only while this page is open: a closed page, a sleeping device, or a tab your browser has put to sleep cannot remind you, and a background tab can be about a minute late. A reminder that would come after its event has started is not shown. With several tabs open, one of them gives each reminder. The page's two addresses (radmagelabs.com and radmageirl.github.io) keep separate settings.

              Privacy

              Settings, stars, adjustments and reminder choices are saved in this browser only. A search you type in Find is not saved at all, and a link to an event carries the event's name and nothing of yours. Two things leave it: the clock check asks this site's own address for the time, and the news panel loads headlines from guildwars2.com, so ArenaNet sees that request. You can turn news off.

              Share and support

              Share passes this timer's address, https://radmagelabs.com/gw2-timer/, to your device's share sheet, or copies it where there is none. It carries nothing of yours. If the timer helps you, you can buy me a coffee; the link opens buymeacoffee.com in a new tab, so reminders keep running in this one.

                Your starred events

                  What reminders can and cannot do

                  • Reminders are for the events you have starred, unless you have hidden them.
                  • They work only while this page is open in a tab. Nothing can remind you when the page or the browser is closed, or while your device sleeps.
                  • A reminder shows only on this page: a notice at the bottom and a count in the tab's title. It is not a notification from your browser or your computer.
                  • In a background tab, or a window covered by your game, a reminder can be up to about a minute late. A browser window you can see, for example on a second monitor, stays on time.
                  • Browsers put tabs they think are unused to sleep, and a sleeping tab cannot remind you. If reminders stop after a while, add this site to your browser's "always keep these sites active" list.
                  • A reminder that would arrive after its event has started is not shown.
                  • With this page open in more than one tab, each reminder is given once, by one of them, so the notice may be in a tab you are not looking at.
                  • This page has two addresses, radmagelabs.com and radmageirl.github.io. Your browser keeps their settings apart, so reminders turned on at one are not on at the other.
                  • Reminders follow the times this page shows. For an event marked Unverified, or one you have adjusted, the reminder says so.

                  Add to calendar

                  A calendar file (.ics) with the scheduled starts you choose, for Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar and others. This page makes the file itself; nothing is sent anywhere.

                  Events

                  Good to know

                  • Each start is its own entry, written in UTC, the game's own time, so your calendar shows it in your time zone, before and after a daylight-saving change.
                  • Scheduled, not live: a map can run early, late, fail or close. An unverified time, or one you set with Adjust, says so in the entry's title.
                  • The file does not update itself when a game schedule changes or when you change Adjust. Download a new file then; entries you imported before stay in your calendar until you delete them.
                  • Importing a file again works differently in each calendar app, and may add a second copy of entries you already have.
                  • Whether a file's alarm is used depends on your calendar app; some use their own default reminder instead. If your phone's calendar app cannot open the file, import it on your calendar's website.
                  • A file holds at most 500 entries.
